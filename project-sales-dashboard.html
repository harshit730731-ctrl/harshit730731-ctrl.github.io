📌 About the Project

This project focuses on analyzing sales data to understand business performance, sales trends and important key metrics. The data is organized and analyzed to create clear reports and useful business insights.

🎯 Project Objective

  • Understand overall sales performance.
  • Identify sales trends and patterns.
  • Find top-performing products.
  • Analyze sales by category and region.
  • Create meaningful business reports.

🛠️ Tools Used

  • 📊 Microsoft Excel
  • 🧹 Data Cleaning
  • 📈 Charts & Visualization
  • 📋 Pivot Tables
  • 🔍 Data Analysis

📊 Dashboard Metrics

💰 Total Sales 📦 Total Orders 🏆 Top Products 📈 Sales Trends 🌍 Regional Sales

💰 Total Sales

This section shows the total sales generated from the dataset.

📦 Total Orders

This section shows the total number of orders in the dataset.

🏆 Top Products

This section highlights the products with the highest sales performance.

📈 Sales Trends

This section explains sales trends and changes over time.

🌍 Regional Sales

This section shows sales performance across different regions.

🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify important sales trends, product performance and regional patterns. These insights can help understand which areas are performing well and where improvement may be required.

✨ Project Features

  • Clean and organized sales dataset
  • Sales performance analysis
  • Interactive-style dashboard reporting
  • Clear charts and visualizations
  • Easy-to-understand business insights
